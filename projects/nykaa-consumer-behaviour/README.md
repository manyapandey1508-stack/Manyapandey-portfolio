# Consumer Behaviour Analysis — Nykaa

## Overview

An independent marketing research project exploring consumer purchasing behaviour across Nykaa's digital and offline touchpoints.

## Research Objective

The study examined how consumer behaviour, psychological triggers, cultural influences, reviews, personalisation, and loyalty mechanisms influence purchasing decisions.

## Key Focus Areas

- Consumer purchasing behaviour
- Digital and offline consumer journeys
- Psychological purchase triggers
- Cultural influence on consumer decisions
- Review-driven trust building
- Campaign personalisation
- Customer retention and loyalty

## Research Approach

The project involved analysing consumer behaviour patterns and translating the findings into marketing-focused strategic recommendations.

## Strategic Recommendations

The research explored opportunities around:

- Personalised marketing campaigns
- Review-led trust building
- Customer loyalty initiatives
- Retention-focused marketing
- Consumer journey optimisation

## Marketing Takeaways

The project demonstrates how consumer research can be translated into practical marketing strategies and business-focused recommendations.

## Project Details

**Type:** Independent Marketing Research  
**Focus:** Consumer Behaviour & Marketing Strategy  
**Duration:** February 2025 – April 2025
